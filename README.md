I am Queen Ugochinyere Alu  (Enya AkpuruenyaChukwu), a resident physician in Nigeria. From medical school to date, I have participated in planning and organizing medical outreaches with roles evolving with my level of expertise.
The complex nature of outreach interactions and the short duration of outreaches remain barriers to proper documentation and record keeping. Every encounter with the healthcare system counts and that is why I have provided this guide for both medical outreach participants and planners to maximize medical outreaches. 

This guide encourages planners to have prewritten documentation so that the time constraints usually seen in some medical outreaches with overwhelming crowds do not adversely affect the quality of care. The guide encourages planners to provide written guides, investigation result sheets, and referral forms.
It also encourages patients to document their outreach care details to maximize their encounters and aid follow-up. 

Outreach Companion is free, the idea is for users to adapt the general ideas it shares to their diary, notes app, or any exercise books. I will add updated features to the framework as time passes and these updates will be free as well.
